# Project Overview

Prime Suspects is a detective-themed party game for teams who want a quick icebreaker on a call. My team was each bringing an icebreaker game, so I made one inspired by Jackbox: the host shares their screen, everyone joins from their phone, and each person answers the same question. Then the answers are revealed one by one, and everyone tries to guess who wrote each one.
